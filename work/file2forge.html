File2Forge · Live website

From a 3D file to a configured order.

A manufacturing storefront with an upload-to-quote tool, interactive 3D preview, production settings, laser-marking options, and an AI material advisor.

All projects

A file becomes an order, step by step.

Customers load STL, OBJ, or 3MF files and inspect their model in the browser. Dimensions and volume feed a configuration flow for material, layer height, infill, color, quantity, and production speed. The quote updates as those choices change.

Customization beyond a product dropdown.

Laser-marking options bring text, serials, QR codes, and logos into the same workflow. The system connects model specifications, marking choices, and order details instead of asking customers to explain everything in an email.

An AI advisor with a defined job.

A conversational material advisor helps customers narrow down print materials for their application. Recommendations are checked against the supported catalog, so the assistant stays within the choices the business can actually offer.
