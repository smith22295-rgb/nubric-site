Complex orders.
A clear way to place them.

Customers build a label, preview their choices, and see the price—or start with a spreadsheet for a bulk order. The system gathers the details the business needs to produce the job.

All projects

Configure it. Preview it. Price it.

Customers choose label dimensions, material, color, mounting, text, and quantity. A visual preview and calculated price make the selected configuration easier to understand before an order is prepared.

Bulk orders without the back-and-forth.

Spreadsheet intake lets customers begin with many labels at once. An editable review step checks the data and gathers specifications in one place, with server-side repricing before order preparation.

Carry the details into production.

The custom backend connects approved configurations to Shopify order preparation and production-file storage. The aim is a consistent set of specifications from the customer’s first choices through the business workflow.

Product guidance, right where questions arise.

An AI-backed chat assistant helps visitors ask about label sizes, materials, pricing and turnaround. It draws on site information and the pricing engine, with an email fallback when it cannot help.
