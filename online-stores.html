Before we set up your store.

What counts as a product?

One catalog item with a supplied title, description, price, category, stock details, and up to 6 variants in total. A variant is one combination, such as a small blue shirt; 3 sizes × 2 colors = 6 variants. Products with conditional options, upload fields, calculated prices, or large variant combinations need a separate review.

How long does it take?

Allow roughly 2–4 weeks for Shop 10 or Shop 25 and 3–6 weeks for larger catalogs after required content, scope, deposit, and access are ready. We agree a realistic start date and review schedule with you.

What if I need custom ordering?

We can add visual customization, file uploads, live estimates, wholesale workflows, and other custom tools. Tell us the workflow first; we’ll quote the store foundation and custom development separately.

What does a revision cover?

A revision round is one combined list of refinements to the agreed design and content. Extra pages, new features, or a new direction after approval are quoted first. Fixing an error in our work never uses a round.

Tell us what you sell.

A product list, an existing shop, or a few examples are enough to begin. We’ll find the right setup together.
