Meet Adam, founder of Nubric

Small-business experience.
Built into every project.

Years of running a business bring a practical perspective to building one online.

Nubric is led by Adam, a business owner and developer with a decade of website-building experience. He has a passion for web and software development—especially turning ideas into useful tools that make running a business easier. As a business owner himself, he understands the work behind a small business: serving customers, keeping things organized and making every investment count.

That experience shapes the work here. Your website should explain what you offer, make it easy to reach you and help your business run more smoothly.

Tell us about your business

From the first website
to the work behind the scenes.

A service business may need a simple site that earns trust. A growing shop may need an easier way to collect an order. Nubric builds both—and the tools in between.

The portfolio includes straightforward service websites, interactive calculators, visual product configurators, 3D file uploads, custom quoting and AI assistants. The technology follows the job your business needs to do.
