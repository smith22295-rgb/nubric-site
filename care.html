Care is optional, billed monthly, and can be canceled before the next billing period. Hosting and provider subscriptions are separate from our time. Canceling care does not remove your website.

What can we change each month?

The everyday updates

Text, photos, opening hours, contact details, service prices, and small layout adjustments. Store Care can also cover straightforward product changes. Checks listed in your plan are included separately from your edit-minute allowance.

Clear limits, no surprise invoice

Unused edit time expires each month. New pages, redesigns, custom coding, integrations, campaigns, and major incidents receive a separate quote. Extra routine edits are $75/hour in 30-minute increments, with your approval first.

We aim to acknowledge requests within one business day. Completion dates depend on the task; monitoring is automated and is not a 24/7 staffed response service.

Built on Cloudflare, where it fits your site

A strong foundation.
Room to build more.

For suitable custom websites, we use Cloudflare’s global platform. It gives us the freedom to build your design and add capabilities as your business grows.

Fast, global delivery

Pages and assets are served across a global network, helping visitors reach your content without relying on a single local server.

Security built into delivery

HTTPS encrypts the connection, and Cloudflare provides DDoS protection. We also configure the site and account access for the agreed project.

Freedom beyond templates

Custom layouts, interactive tools, serverless functions, storage, and AI integrations can be added when the project needs them. Each added system has its own scope.

Your accounts. Clear costs.

Suitable static sites can use a free hosting tier within provider limits. Paid usage, forms, storage, or services may introduce costs; we agree those with you. Domains renew separately.

Shopify stores are hosted by Shopify and require its subscription. Platform choice depends on the project. See Cloudflare’s developer platform, HTTPS certificates, and DDoS protection.

Practical protection for your business.

Account & brand ownership

Domain registration in your account, access through invitations where supported, multifactor authentication guidance, and email sender authentication help protect your business identity and access.

A recoverable website

We keep source changes in version control and hand over the agreed deliverables after payment. Custom application data and Shopify backup needs are reviewed separately; site-source history is not a backup of every customer record.
