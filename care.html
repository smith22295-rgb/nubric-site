The practical details.

What if I need more time or a bigger change?

Extra routine edits are $75/hour in 30-minute increments, approved first. New pages, redesigns, custom features, integrations, campaigns, and major incidents receive a separate quote. Unused edit time does not roll over.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. You can request one-off updates instead. We confirm your site is a fit before care starts; existing repairs, CMS/plugin maintenance, databases and custom backends need their own scope. Care is billed monthly and can be canceled before the next period. Domain, email, Shopify, and other provider charges remain separate.

What do monitoring and backups cover?

Uptime alerts are automated; this is not a 24/7 staffed response service. We review the items listed in your plan and keep website source changes in version history. Database, customer-record, and Shopify backup needs are scoped separately.

How do you help protect my accounts?

Your domain and service accounts stay in your name. We use collaborator invitations where supported, offer multifactor authentication guidance, and configure email sender authentication when email setup is included. Website source files and agreed deliverables are handed over after payment.
