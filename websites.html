Website questions, answered.

What do I need to send you?

Send your business details, ideas and any photos or references. Rough notes are welcome. We write the copy for your agreed pages, or use your own text. You approve the wording and design before launch.

Can you create the style I want?

Yes. Share the look you want. Our custom websites offer full design control. Starter uses adaptable sections; more elaborate layouts, animations and interactive features receive a separate quote.

How soon can my website go live?

Typical build times: Starter, 5–7 business days; Standard, 1–2 weeks; Website Pro, 2–3 weeks. We confirm availability before booking. Work starts once your plan, required information, access and start payment are ready. Delayed feedback can extend the schedule.

What if I want changes to the first design?

Starter includes 2 website revision rounds; Standard and Website Pro include 3. A round is one combined feedback list within the agreed direction. New pages, features or design directions are quoted separately. Corrections to our work do not use a round.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes, if we include an editing system in your quote. Tell us what you want to update before we build; a visual editor is an optional extra. Or let Nubric handle updates through one-off edits or monthly care.

Will I need to pay a monthly fee?

No Nubric subscription is required. Domain, business email and paid provider services are billed separately and listed in your quote. Monthly care is optional, and one-off paid edits are available when needed.

Will customers be able to find me on Google?

Yes—we get your website set up to be found on Google, with search-friendly page titles, descriptions, a sitemap and Search Console guidance. Google Business Profile support and ongoing SEO are available separately.
