Your questions, answered.

Can you design beyond a preset theme?

Yes. Our custom websites give us full design control over layouts, colors, typography and interactions. Bring the look you have in mind, or let us develop it with you. Starter uses adaptable sections to keep the build affordable; bespoke layouts and advanced features are quoted to match your vision.

Do I need polished copy and organized files?

No. Start with what you have: notes, photos, links, screenshots, or a list of services. We help organize it. The package defines how much editing or writing is included; we flag extra writing, photography, or research before quoting it.

How do revisions and timing work?

A revision round is one combined list of refinements to the agreed design and content. Extra pages, new features, or a new direction after approval are quoted first. Fixing an error in our work never uses a round.

Typical build windows after scope, required assets, and deposit: Starter 5–7 business days, Standard 1–2 weeks, Website Pro 2–3 weeks. Your confirmed schedule accounts for current availability and review time.

What costs extra?

Logo Essentials is included in Website Pro; it is $249 with Starter or Standard. Brand Kit is a $200 upgrade when Logo Essentials is included. Online checkout, copy beyond your allowance, ongoing SEO campaigns, custom integrations, and advanced tools. Domain and email renewals and any paid provider plans are paid through your own accounts. Optional care starts at $39/month.
