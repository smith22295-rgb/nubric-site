Your questions, answered.

Am I limited to a template?

Bring any visual references you like. We can explore bold, minimal, playful, or highly bespoke designs. QuickStart uses efficient, adaptable sections to stay affordable; fully bespoke layouts and interactions receive a suitable scope. Your direction leads the design.

Do I need polished copy and organized files?

No. Start with what you have: notes, photos, links, screenshots, or a list of services. We help organize it. The package defines how much editing or writing is included; we flag extra writing, photography, or research before quoting it.

How do revisions and timing work?

A revision round is one combined list of refinements to the agreed design and content. Extra pages, new features, or a new direction after approval are quoted first. Fixing an error in our work never uses a round.

Typical build windows after scope, required assets, and deposit: QuickStart 5–7 business days, Standard 1–2 weeks, Business Plus 2–3 weeks. Your confirmed schedule accounts for current availability and review time.

What costs extra?

New branding, online checkout, copy beyond your allowance, ongoing SEO campaigns, custom integrations, and advanced tools. Domain and email renewals and any paid provider plans are paid through your own accounts. Optional care starts at $39/month.
