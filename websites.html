A few things to know.

Can you create the look I have in mind?

Yes. Our custom websites give us full design control. Share the style, examples or ideas you love. Starter uses adaptable sections to keep it affordable; bespoke layouts and advanced effects are quoted to fit your vision.

Can I edit the website myself?

Tell us before we quote. These packages use code-based websites; a visual editor is not included by default. We can quote a content editor for the areas you want to update, or handle edits for you through optional care or one-off updates. You receive the source files either way.

What do I need to provide?

Send your text, photos, references and rough notes. We help organize them. Standard includes light editing; Website Pro includes drafting from your facts. Extra writing, photography, research and licensed assets are agreed separately.

How long does it take, and how do revisions work?

Starter: 5–7 business days. Standard: 1–2 weeks. Website Pro: 2–3 weeks. We confirm availability and dates after reviewing your scope, content, access and deposit.

A revision round is one combined list of refinements to the agreed design and content. Extra pages, new features, or a new direction after approval are quoted first. Fixing an error in our work never uses a round.

What costs extra?

Domain registration, email, paid provider plans and optional care are separate. Logo Essentials is $249 with Starter or Standard, and included in Website Pro. Brand Kit is a $200 upgrade when a logo is included. Shops, custom tools, advanced effects and ongoing SEO need their own quote.
