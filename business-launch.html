A little more detail, when you need it.

How is the first year of domain and email paid?

To keep ownership and billing in your name, you pay the selected providers directly. We deduct those first-year costs from your Nubric bill, up to $20 for one domain and $84 for one mailbox. You do not pay that amount twice. For example, a $20 domain + an $84 mailbox + a $595 Nubric invoice = $699 total before tax.

We confirm the plan, renewal price, and any annual commitment before purchase. Unused allowances have no cash value. Existing mailbox migration and additional accounts are separate.

What is included in the website and logo?

You get the QuickStart website: up to 4 pages, your supplied content organized, one contact form with up to 5 fields, mobile layouts, basic search setup, domain connection, and suitable Cloudflare hosting with HTTPS. It includes a 20-minute walkthrough and 14 days of launch bug fixes.

Logo Essentials includes 1 direction, 2 revision rounds, SVG and PNG files, light and dark versions, colors, font recommendations, and a social profile image. Email setup includes a signature, supported sender authentication, and a send/receive test.

What do I need to do?

Send your business name, services, photos, and ideas—even if they are scattered. We organize what you provide, guide the account setup, and send previews for your feedback. You keep control of account approvals and payment details. Extensive copywriting, photography, or naming work can be added by quote.

How soon can we launch?

Usually 1–3 weeks once scope, deposit, required information, and access are ready. We confirm your start date and keep you updated as the work takes shape.

How do revisions work?

The website and logo each include 2 rounds of feedback. A round is one combined list of refinements within the agreed direction. New features or a fresh direction are quoted first; fixing an error in our work never uses a round.

What if my idea needs more?

We can add pages, a shop, custom tools, animation, or more branding. We’ll put together a clear quote, or help you launch in stages. Business Plus at $1,499 is our larger website package with more writing help, forms, and a booking embed; branding and email setup are separate.
