Before we begin.

What do I need to have ready?

Your business name, what you offer, who you serve, and any existing text, photos, or ideas. Rough notes are welcome. We organize supplied content; extensive writing, photography, or naming work is a separate scope.

How long will it take?

Allow around 1–3 weeks after scope, deposit, required information, and access are ready. We confirm a realistic schedule before you commit.

What if I need more?

More pages, additional logo directions, online checkout, calculators, bespoke animations, and custom ordering systems are quoted separately. We can build in phases.

How do the revision rounds work?

A revision round is one collected list of changes to the agreed design and content. New pages, a new direction after approval, and new features are quoted before we add them. Corrections to our implementation do not use your revision allowance.

Logo refinements stay within the selected direction. The website and logo have separate allowances of two rounds each.
