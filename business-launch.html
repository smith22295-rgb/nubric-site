What if my domain or email costs less?

The package stays $699. We subtract what you actually pay providers, up to $20 for the domain and $84 for the mailbox, from your Nubric invoice. Lower provider costs mean a larger Nubric portion. You’ll see both amounts in your quote before booking.

After the first year.

Domain and email renew directly with providers. Website care is optional; no Nubric subscription is required.

The details, when you need them.

Exactly what is included?

Starter includes up to 4 pages with copy written for you or supplied by you, one form with up to 5 fields, mobile layouts, basic search setup, suitable Cloudflare hosting with HTTPS, a 20-minute walkthrough and 14 days of launch bug fixes.

Logo Essentials includes 1 direction, SVG and PNG files, light/dark versions, colors, font recommendations and a social profile image. Business card design covers 1 double-sided layout for 1 person, a print-ready PDF and a preview file; printing and shipping are separate. Email setup includes a signature, sender authentication and sending/receiving tests.

How soon can we launch?

Usually 1–3 weeks after the scope, deposit, required content and access are ready. We confirm your start date and share progress during the build.

How do revisions work?

2 website rounds and 2 logo rounds, tracked separately. Card refinements are included in the logo rounds. Each round is one combined list of refinements to the agreed direction. Corrections to our work do not use a round. Brand Kit is a $200 upgrade with 3 brand rounds in total, covering the chosen logo and card design.

Can I add more, or use what I already have?

Yes. We can quote extra pages, a content editor, custom tools or a store. Existing mailbox migration, extra mailboxes, premium domains and paid hosting features are separate. If you already have the basics, choose a website package and only the extra help you need.
