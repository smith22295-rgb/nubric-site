From an idea to an identity.

Your business name, what you do, and a few things you like are enough to begin. Rough sketches and references help us understand what you have in mind.

We handle the design and files.

We review the direction together, refine it, and finish with files you can use. Your chosen direction guides the refinements and final deliverables.

Logo scope, timing and revisions.

How long will it take?

Usually 1–2 weeks after the brief, deposit, and required information are ready. We confirm the schedule before we begin.

What does a revision include?

One combined list of refinements to color, type, spacing, or the chosen mark. Additional concepts or a new direction after selection are quoted first. Fixing an error in our work does not use a revision round.

Are printed business cards included?

Brand Kit includes one double-sided card design for one person, a print-ready PDF and a preview file. We prepare the artwork for your chosen printer and refine it within your 3 brand revision rounds. Printing, shipping and additional names or layouts are separate. Business Launch also includes card design; standalone business card design is $99.

Can I upgrade a logo included in my website package?

Logo Essentials is included in Website Pro and Business Launch. Upgrade to Brand Kit for $200 extra: two initial directions in total, business card design, a fuller asset set and three brand revision rounds. We agree the upgrade before design starts.

Can you help protect my brand?

We check basic visual similarity and consider a consistent domain and social handle. Legal trademark clearance, registration, detailed illustration, naming research, and ongoing impersonation monitoring are separate specialist work.
