A business launch is different from Business Plus.

Starting a new business?

Business Launch ($699) combines the smaller QuickStart website with a starter logo and domain/email setup. It is for getting the essentials in place.

Need a larger website?

Business Plus ($1,499) is a website package with up to 10 pages, copy drafting, more forms, and booking integration. Branding and email are separate.

Domain & email, explained

Your domain is your address, such as yourbusiness.com. Your mailbox is an account such as hello@yourbusiness.com. They are separate services in accounts you control.

First year & renewals

Business Launch includes invoice credits for actual first-year costs, capped at $20 for one domain and $84 for one mailbox, before tax. Provider bills are paid by you; unused credit is not cash. We confirm the selected plan before purchase.

Premium domains, extra mailboxes, higher plans, and migrations cost extra. Renewals are paid directly at the provider’s then-current rate. A quoted annual email plan may require a one-year commitment.

A stronger brand foundation

We consider domain and handle consistency, make basic visual-similarity checks, set up sender authentication where supported, and help keep account ownership with your business.

Practical scope

Simple logo design and usable brand files are included as listed. Naming research, detailed illustration, a full brand strategy, trademark clearance, registration, and ongoing impersonation monitoring are separate specialist work.

A few details before we begin.

Can I add email without a launch package?

Yes. Setup is $49 for one existing domain and one mailbox, including provider connection, supported sender authentication, and a send/receive test. Provider subscription and migration are separate.

What do logo revisions include?

A collected round of adjustments to the selected direction: color, type, spacing, or refinement of the mark. Additional initial concepts or a fresh direction after selection receive a separate quote. Brand Kit includes 2 initial directions; Logo Essentials and Business Launch include 1.

How long will it take?

Allow around 1–2 weeks for a logo and 1–3 weeks for a launch bundle after the brief, deposit, and needed assets are ready. We agree timing before you commit.

What should I send?

Your business name, what you do, who you serve, and anything that feels right or wrong for your brand. Images, rough sketches, favorite sites, and unorganized notes are all useful. You don’t need design terminology.

Your ideas are a good place to start.

Send the notes, links, screenshots, and half-formed ideas. I’ll help turn them into a clear plan.
