Know what you’re getting.

What counts as a revision?

One collected round of color, type, spacing, or mark refinements within the selected direction. Extra initial concepts or a fresh direction after selection receive a separate quote. Corrections to our implementation do not use your revision allowance.

What about brand protection?

We consider domain and handle consistency and make basic visual-similarity checks. Naming research, detailed illustration, a full brand strategy, trademark clearance or registration, and ongoing impersonation monitoring are separate specialist work.

What should I send, and how long will it take?

Send your name, what you do, your audience, and references or rough sketches. Allow around 1–2 weeks after the brief, deposit, and required information are ready; we confirm availability and timing first.

Business Launch is $699 and brings a QuickStart website, Logo Essentials, domain connection, and one mailbox setup together. For updates to an existing site or help getting connected, see other services.
