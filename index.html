Before we
get started.

Clear answers to the practical questions.

Will I own my website?

Your accounts and domain belong to you. Custom deliverables are handed over after full payment, subject to any third-party licenses. Shopify themes and apps keep their own licensing rules.

Are there monthly fees?

Nubric care plans are optional. Business Launch includes capped credits for the first year of one domain and one email mailbox. Renewals are paid directly to the providers at their current rates. Website-only packages, Shopify, and other paid services have separate provider charges. We explain the costs before work starts.

What do I need to provide?

Your chosen business name, final text, photos, business and contact details, and product data for a shop. Website-only packages use your supplied logo; Business Launch creates a starter logo. Where supported, you grant access through account invitations.

How long does a website take?

QuickStart targets 5–7 business days after all content and the deposit arrive, subject to an agreed start date. Larger websites, shops, and custom tools receive a project-specific schedule.

Can I add more products later?

Yes. The shop package covers the products we load for launch. You can add more yourself, or ask Nubric for an additional loading quote.

What happens after launch?

Handoff is included. We correct defects in the agreed build for 14 days after launch. Later edits can use an optional care plan or a separate quote.

Can you build a custom ordering or quoting tool?

Yes. Tell us the options, pricing rules, and how an order should reach your business. We scope that work separately from standard website and Shopify setup packages.

Who will I work with?

You’ll work directly with the person building your website. Nubric is owner-operated, and I handle project communication, development, and launch.
