A content structure, interactive tools, reusable article layouts, labeled ad positions, affiliate-link placements, and the technical setup for the providers you choose. The proposal spells out how many tools, pages, and integrations are included.

Approval comes from the provider

Google AdSense reviews eligibility, original useful content, and policy compliance. Creating ad spaces does not mean an ad network has approved the site or will fill every space. Audience, content, ad availability, and provider rules all matter.

Amazon Associates is an affiliate program: links can earn commissions from qualifying purchases. It is different from an automatically filled display-ad slot. Amazon currently reviews an application after three qualifying sales within the first 180 days.
