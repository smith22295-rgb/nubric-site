Can you add a tool to my current website?

Often, yes. We review your platform and the task you want to improve, then recommend a suitable integration or a custom build.

Can an assistant use my business information?

Yes. We agree the content it can draw on, the questions it should handle and the route to personal help. Access and data handling are part of the project plan.

What happens after launch?

Your proposal sets out launch support, handoff and any ongoing maintenance. We can support the tool after launch or prepare it for an agreed handover.

Can a content website include advertising?

Yes. We can build ad placements and affiliate connections into the layout. Eligibility depends on the provider, your content and your audience. Ad management, content creation and audience growth are separately scoped; approval and earnings are not guaranteed.
