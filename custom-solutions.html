How advertising fits in

The build and ongoing audience growth are separate parts of the plan. Your provider accounts stay yours.

When can a website start showing ads?

It depends on the provider, your content, and your audience. Allow 3–6 months to publish, promote, and measure as an initial planning window—not an approval rule or promise. Google indexing and time alone do not guarantee approval.

Google AdSense reviews eligibility and content. Amazon Associates is an affiliate program, not a display-ad network; its application review follows three qualifying sales in the first 180 days.

What happens after the website is built?

You control the provider accounts and publish or commission content. Ongoing content creation, audience growth, SEO campaigns, and ad management are separate unless included in your proposal. We can set up the site for search discovery; Google decides what it indexes.
