Can this work with my existing website?

Often, yes. We review your platform and recommend how to add the tool or connect the services you already use.

Can an assistant use my business information?

Yes. We define its knowledge, allowed actions and handoff to your team. Privacy and access are included in the project plan.

What about support after launch?

We agree launch support and handoff in your proposal. Ongoing care is available when you want help maintaining or improving the tool.

Can a content website earn from advertising?

We can build advertising and affiliate placements into your website. Provider approval depends on your content and audience; approval and earnings are not guaranteed.
