HourMark

Privacy Policy

Effective: April 9, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 11, 2026

HourMark is built for 1099 contractors and the businesses that pay them. We take privacy seriously — your data stays yours. We do not sell your data, ever.

1. Who We Are

Nubric LLC ("we," "our," "us") operates the HourMark mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information. You can reach us at info@nubric.dev.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and display name from your authentication provider. This is used solely to create and secure your account.

Data You Enter

HourMark stores contractor names, addresses, hourly rates, pay amounts, job names, and work entries that you enter into the app. If you sign in with Apple or Google, these records sync through Firebase. In local mode, these records remain on your iPhone unless you choose to export or share them.

Tax Identification Numbers (TINs/SSNs/EINs)

TINs are stored in encrypted on-device secure storage (iOS Keychain) and excluded from Firebase sync. They are masked in printed worksheets and omitted from CSV reports. The full JSON export includes unmasked tax IDs; the app warns you to store that file securely. If you share the file with a service or another person, that destination receives its contents. This export is a copy of your records; the app does not currently include an import workflow.

Analytics Data

The current iOS app does not send screen or feature events through Firebase Analytics. The RevenueCat subscription SDK receives installation/customer identifiers, app and platform information, purchase events, and subscription status to provide purchases, restores, entitlement checks, and subscription metrics. These services are used in local mode too; local mode refers to where your contractor and work records are stored.

Feedback

If you choose to submit feedback through the app, we collect:

  • Your message (up to 500 characters)
  • An optional star rating
  • Your platform and app version
  • Your app user ID, which can be associated with your signed-in account

Subscription and Purchase Information

HourMark offers optional paid subscriptions ("HourMark Pro") through the Apple App Store. When you subscribe:

  • Your payment is processed by Apple. We never see, receive, or store your credit card or payment details.
  • We use RevenueCat to validate subscription receipts, restore purchases, and maintain Pro access. Signed-in purchases use your app account identifier; local-mode purchases use a RevenueCat installation/customer identifier. Subscription records can be associated with your app account and are not necessarily anonymous.
  • We do not receive your name, email, address, or payment method from the App Store purchase.
  • RevenueCat's privacy practices are described at revenuecat.com/privacy.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • App functionality — To store and sync your contractor and pay tracking data
  • Subscriptions — To validate purchases, restore access, and understand subscription activity
  • Feedback — To respond to user suggestions and fix issues

We do not sell your records or use them for advertising. The service providers listed below process the information needed to operate the app. Exports are shared only with destinations you select.

4. Tracking Disclosure

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). Subscription services use the identifiers described above to maintain purchase access.

5. Third-Party Services

HourMark uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — Optional authentication and Firestore sync, plus submitted feedback. Native Firebase Analytics events are not enabled. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Sign-In (when available) — See Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store (In-App Purchases) — Processes HourMark Pro subscription payments. See Apple Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — Manages subscription entitlements and syncs Pro status across devices. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

These services process data as described in their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Processing Location

Signed-in cloud records are stored in the configured U.S. Firestore region. Apple and RevenueCat may process billing and technical information in other locations as described in their policies. Local-mode contractor and work records are stored on your iPhone.

7. Data Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • TINs are encrypted at rest using platform secure storage
  • Firestore access is restricted so each user can only access their own data
  • We do not store passwords — authentication is handled by Google/Apple

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your data is retained as long as your account is active
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account
  • Deletion removes all data from Firebase and clears local storage
  • Local-only mode users can delete all data from Settings → Delete All Local Data

9. Children's Privacy

HourMark is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
