Scope, access and payment.

Can I combine these services?

Yes. Add them to a website package or book them on their own. If you need a website, logo, business cards, domain and email together, Business Launch is $699.

What about extra mailboxes or an email move?

The $49 setup covers one existing domain and one new mailbox. Registration, subscriptions, additional accounts, email migration, and existing delivery problems are quoted separately. Existing domain records are reviewed before changes.

How do access and payment work?

We use collaborator invitations where supported, so you keep control of your accounts. Please do not email passwords. Hourly work uses an approved scope and time budget; fixed-price work uses 50% to begin and the balance after approval. Prices are in USD before applicable tax. Provider subscriptions and usage are separate.

What if an update needs correcting?

Corrections to our implementation are included. New requests beyond the agreed update receive a price before work continues.
