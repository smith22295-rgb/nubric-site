Your style. Your business. Your say.

Bring the ambitious ideas, too.

There is no house style you have to fit. Bold, minimal, playful, or something you have not seen before—show us what you love. We’ll explain what fits your package and quote any custom design or features.

Trust is part of the work.

Accounts stay in your name. We use collaborator access where available and request only what the work needs. Your project information is used to deliver the agreed service, with third-party tools and data needs discussed when relevant.

A clear way to give feedback.

How many revisions do I get?

Starter includes 2 rounds; Standard and Website Pro include 3. Store and logo allowances are shown with each package.

A revision round is one combined list of refinements to the agreed design and content. Extra pages, new features, or a new direction after approval are quoted first. Fixing an error in our work never uses a round.

How should I send feedback?

One combined list per round works best. Aim to send it within 5 business days; if you need longer, let us know and we’ll adjust the schedule. You approve extra work before it is billed.

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. Tell us what your customers need and what you want to achieve. We’ll guide the choices, explain any account approvals, and handle the design, development, and launch setup.
