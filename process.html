The process

Bring the ideas.
We’ll guide the next steps.

You do not need a polished brief. Send the notes, photos, favorite websites, and half-formed ideas. The more we understand your vision, the better we can bring it to life.

A clear process, from idea to launch.

Design and development stay with Nubric. Your scope, approvals and progress updates are coordinated throughout the project.

Expect plain-English answers, previews you can explore, and a progress update at least weekly during your build. We aim to reply to email within one business day.

Tell us your idea

  1. Talk it through.

    We start with your business, your customers, and what you want the website to do. A free consultation helps us find the right package or custom approach.

  2. Know the plan and price.

    You review and sign a plan covering the features, price, revisions and schedule. We identify anything needed from you. For fixed-price projects, the agreed start payment is usually 50%. Hourly updates and monthly care have their own billing terms.

  3. See it take shape.

    We share a working preview, explain the choices, and refine it with your feedback. You stay involved without having to manage the technical details.

  4. Approve it. Launch it.

    We check the mobile layout, links, forms, and agreed features. After approval and final payment, we handle launch and walk you through your website.

  5. Keep a direct line.

    Launch bug fixes are included for 14 days. After that, choose optional care or ask for an update when you need one. Your website and accounts stay yours.

Your style. Your business. Your say.

Bring the ambitious ideas, too.

There is no house style you have to fit. Bold, minimal, playful, or something you have not seen before—show us what you love. We’ll explain what fits your package and quote any custom design or features.

Trust is part of the work.

Accounts stay in your name. We use collaborator access where available and request only what the work needs. Your project information is used to deliver the agreed service, with third-party tools and data needs discussed when relevant.

A clear way to give feedback.

How many revisions do I get?

Starter includes 2 rounds; Standard and Website Pro include 3. Store and logo allowances are shown with each package.

A revision round is one combined list of refinements to the agreed design and content. Extra pages, new features, or a new direction after approval are quoted first. Fixing an error in our work never uses a round.

How should I send feedback?

One combined list per round works best. Aim to send it within 5 business days; if you need longer, let us know and we’ll adjust the schedule. You approve extra work before it is billed.

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. Tell us what your customers need and what you want to achieve. We’ll guide the choices, explain any account approvals, and handle the design, development, and launch setup.
