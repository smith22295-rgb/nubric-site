The process

Bring the ideas.
We’ll work out the details.

You do not need a polished brief to start. Send everything that helps me understand your vision, even if it’s scattered, unfinished, or hard to explain.

Messy notes are welcome.

Favorite websites. Phone photos. A logo sketch. A list of services. A feature you saw somewhere. Things you really don’t like.

Share as much as you can. I’ll ask useful questions, organize the information, and help turn it into a site that makes sense to your customers.

Tell me your idea

  1. Talk through the vision.

    We discuss your business, customers, style, budget, and what visitors should be able to do. You work directly with the owner and builder throughout.

  2. Agree the plan & price.

    You get a written scope: pages, features, content help, revision rounds, provider costs, and target dates. We confirm what is needed from you. A 50% deposit books the agreed work.

  3. Build, review, refine.

    I share a working preview, explain progress, and gather your feedback. You can see the direction taking shape instead of waiting for a surprise at the end.

  4. Launch with confidence.

    We check mobile layouts, links, forms, and the agreed features. After your approval and final payment, we launch, hand over access, and walk through what you need to know.

  5. Keep a direct line.

    Launch defects are corrected for 14 days. For new edits, choose optional monthly care or ask for a one-off quote. Your website and accounts stay yours.

Creative freedom, with a plan you can trust.

No prescribed visual style.

Bring your references and ambitions. We can design quiet, colorful, editorial, technical, or something entirely your own. We build with flexible tools and code, so your ideas can go beyond a page-builder template.

The right package depends on the work involved. Highly bespoke layouts, animation, and custom functionality get their own scope, with the cost agreed first.

Communication is part of the service.

One person to contact. Plain-English answers. A preview you can explore. I aim to acknowledge messages within one business day and provide a progress update at least weekly while your build is active.

If something affects scope or timing, we discuss it before moving ahead. Call anytime at 941-451-6796 or email info@nubric.dev for a free consultation. If I miss your call, leave a message and I’ll get back to you.

Reviews that move the project forward.

A revision round is one collected list of changes to the agreed design and content. New pages, a new direction after approval, and new features are quoted before we add them. Corrections to our implementation do not use your revision allowance.

QuickStart includes 2 rounds; Standard and Business Plus include 3. Store packages include 2 or 3 according to tier. Logo allowances are listed separately from website allowances.

Send one combined feedback list per round, ideally within 5 business days. If you need longer, tell me and we’ll update the schedule. You approve extra work in writing before it is billed.
