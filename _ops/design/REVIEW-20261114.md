# Nubric design review, November 14, 2026

The previous homepage introduced the offer repeatedly through a service catalog, an SEO section, a project switcher, a full visualizer, the studio story and a separate process section. Its mobile layout asked visitors to scroll through several competing presentations before reaching contact. Pricing pages repeated full feature lists before comparison, and the service directory treated a launch bundle as equivalent to a website type.

The implemented direction uses Bricolage Grotesque headings, locally hosted fonts, restrained orange actions and the established navy. A compact interactive hero leads into three service choices. Business Launch has a distinct bundle position. Two real projects lead into client feedback, a brief process and consultation. The family portrait and studio story live on About. Full interactive tools remain on their dedicated page. The album portfolio remains the main work browser.

Two homepage compositions were rendered on desktop and mobile. The split composition brings services into view sooner than the full-width gallery composition and was selected. No new business claims, prices or geographic coverage were introduced.

Package cards show price, page allowance and a distinguishing inclusion before optional details. The service directory groups branding, business cards, setup, care and search visibility. Business Launch retains its existing combined-price calculation in expandable payment details. Contact forms start with name, email and project description, with optional business, package and timing details. A selected package expands those details automatically.

Browser review covered 17 routes at 1440, 768, 390 and 320 pixels. No horizontal overflow, broken visible images or JavaScript errors were found. Checks exercised the menu and Escape behavior, day/evening control, portfolio navigation and drag, guided conversation, and nine service selections into the contact form. Core homepage, form and portfolio paths were also checked with JavaScript disabled. Existing 40 gallery gesture checks passed.

All 40 public HTML pages passed internal link, anchor and duplicate-ID checks. Existing metadata and structured data were compared with the prior release. Form fields, payment destinations, package prices, the app directory and both apps’ policy pages were checked for preservation. The security checker passed. These are browser and source reviews, with customer response to the redesign still to be measured.
