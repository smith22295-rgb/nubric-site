# Nubric web design

Use this workflow to make intentional design decisions and carry them consistently through a website. It complements frontend implementation guidance. It does not authorize publishing, contacting clients, or writing Client Desk notes.

## Establish the brief

Read the project's brand brief and applicable AGENTS.md. Identify the intended visitor, primary action, business offer, existing assets, and explicit preferences to preserve. Each client needs their own visual identity; Nubric's colors and typography are not defaults for their sites.

For major design work, inspect the current site at desktop and mobile sizes. Record concrete visual problems and the impact on visitors. Separate observations from assumptions. Select a small number of relevant references and identify the particular ideas worth learning from, without copying layouts, assets or claims.

## Choose a direction

For a substantial redesign, develop two meaningfully different compositions using real headings, imagery, prices and calls to action. Compare a representative desktop and mobile view. Explain which direction best serves the brief; use existing authorization and judgment rather than introducing a mandatory approval gate.

Create a compact brand brief covering palette roles, typography, content widths, spacing, image treatment, navigation, and motion. Specify the behavior of mobile layouts, not just their smaller font sizes. An ordinary correction does not need new concepts or a new brief.

## Build with restraint

- Give the strongest proof room to be seen. Avoid miniature screenshots surrounded by unnecessary device chrome or decorative containers.
- Use clear service names. Show the main decision first and reveal detailed comparisons or FAQs when useful.
- Make each section earn its place. Combine repeated pitches and move deep explanations to the appropriate service or project page.
- Use consistent component rules and edit their source. Avoid accumulating successive overrides that fight earlier styles. Retain immutable asset versions where needed for cached pages.
- Treat hover as an enhancement: destinations and actions must be evident on touch devices.
- Tie animation to a visitor's action or a specific moment. Avoid autoplay carousels, scroll hijacking and blanket entrance effects. Respect reduced motion and keep content available without animation.
- Preserve commercial terms, real portfolio claims, legal/app links, form protections and analytics consent. Escalate actual conflicts to Adam; do not invent testimonials or promises.

## Review separately from testing

First test behavior: navigation, forms without sending test inquiries, tabs, galleries, links and relevant business calculations. Then critique the rendered design: hierarchy, readable text, image framing, consistent spacing, and clear next steps.

Inspect representative desktop, intermediate and narrow mobile widths. Check keyboard focus, touch alternatives, overflow, reduced motion, loading states, image failures, and the no-JavaScript baseline where applicable. Fix the most consequential findings and capture final screenshots. A passing script or a self-assigned design score does not establish visual quality.

Use the rubric in [references/review.md](references/review.md) when doing a full design pass. Record what was actually checked and remaining limitations. Real customer testing requires people: do not describe simulated journeys as user research.

## Carry decisions forward

Keep the project's brand brief with its source and link to it from AGENTS.md. Record lasting preferences there when Adam confirms them. Keep new knowledge concise and specific. Work notes in Client Desk remain manual and require an explicit request.
