Sixty4 Racing

Privacy Policy

Effective: June 7, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 7, 2026

Sixty4 Racing is built by Nubric ("we," "our," "us"). Short version: the app works fully with no account and no cloud — everything stays on your phone. Signing in is optional and only adds cloud backup so your garage syncs across your devices. This policy spells out the details in plain English. Questions? info@nubric.dev.

1. Who this policy applies to

Anyone who uses the Sixty4 Racing app on iOS (and Android, when released). Sixty4 Racing is a companion app for the Sixty4 Racing speed-trap timer, which clocks 1:64 scale diecast cars. The product site is sixty4racing.com.

2. Accounts & sign-in are optional

You do not need an account to use Sixty4 Racing — the garage, runs, and timing all work signed out. If you choose to sign in, you can use Sign in with Apple or Google. When you do, we receive:

  • Your email address and a user ID from the provider. If you use Sign in with Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever receive Apple's private relay address — not your real one.

We use this only to identify your account and sync your data. We don't ask for your name, contacts, or anything else, and we don't post anywhere on your behalf.

3. What the app stores on your device

Whether or not you sign in, your data lives in a local database on your phone:

  • Your garage — cars, run history, lap and elapsed-time records, top speeds
  • Your paired devices — names and roles you assign to your Sixty4 units
  • Your preferences and any car photos you add (see below)

Signed out, this never leaves your device. You can erase it anytime in Settings → Your data → Delete local data, or by uninstalling.

4. Cloud backup & sync (only when signed in)

If you sign in, your garage and runs are backed up to our cloud, hosted on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), and tied to your account so they sync across your own devices. This data is private to your account — it is not public, not shared with other users, and never sold. If you sign out or delete your account, the cloud copy is removed (see Section 9).

5. Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth Low Energy only to connect to your nearby Sixty4 timing hardware and receive run results. We do not collect or transmit your location. On Android, Bluetooth scanning is set to "never for location," and the app does not request location permission.

6. Photos

If you add a photo to a car, the app opens your system photo picker so you choose one image, stored locally as part of your garage. The app does not request full photo-library access — only the single image you pick. (If cloud sync is on, photos you add may sync to your private cloud backup like the rest of your garage.)

7. What leaves your device

  • Always: Expo (EAS Update) for over-the-air app updates, and Apple/Google for app distribution and anonymized, standard crash reporting. No personal data is sent.
  • Only when you sign in: Google Firebase Authentication (your sign-in) and Cloud Firestore (your synced garage), and the Google/Apple sign-in flow itself.

We use no third-party advertising trackers, no analytics SDKs, and we do not participate in Apple's IDFA tracking. We do not sell data. Period.

8. The car catalog

The searchable make/model list is bundled inside the app — not fetched from the network. It comes from Wikidata (CC0 public-domain data) plus a curated list of brand-original casting names. Those names (e.g. Hot Wheels, Matchbox, Johnny Lightning) are trademarks of their respective owners, shown only to help you identify a car; Nubric and Sixty4 Racing are not affiliated with or endorsed by those companies.

10. Your rights & controls

  • Delete your account + cloud data: Settings → Account → Delete account — this permanently removes your sign-in and all synced cloud data. See Clearing Your Data.
  • Delete on-device data: Settings → Your data → Delete local data.
  • Uninstall to remove all local data; revoke Bluetooth or Photos permission any time in system settings.

If you live in California (CCPA), the EU/UK (GDPR), or elsewhere with privacy rights: signed out, we hold nothing about you. Signed in, we hold your email, a user ID, and your synced garage — all of which you can delete with one tap (above) or by emailing info@nubric.dev.

11. Children

Sixty4 Racing is rated 4+ and contains no user-generated content, social features, or messaging. Accounts are optional; a parent or guardian should set up any account for a child under 13. The app is not part of Apple's Kids Category.

12. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds new data collection (e.g. leaderboards or sharing), we will update this policy and notify users in-app before enabling those features. The "Last updated" date will change.
