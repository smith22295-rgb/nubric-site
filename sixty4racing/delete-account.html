Delete Your Account

Effective: June 7, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 7, 2026

An account in Sixty4 Racing is optional. If you signed in (with Apple or Google), you can delete your account and all of its cloud data at any time — from inside the app. If you never signed in, there is no account; only local data on your device.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Sixty4 Racing on your phone
  2. Go to Settings → Account
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm

This permanently deletes your sign-in and all cloud-synced data (your garage, runs, and records) from our servers. It cannot be undone. (You may be asked to sign in again first, which is a standard security step before deletion.)

2. If you never signed in

There is no account to delete — we hold nothing about you. To clear the app's on-device data, use Settings → Your data → Delete local data, or uninstall the app.

3. What deletion removes — and what we keep

Deleting your account removes your authentication record and your cloud garage from Google Firebase. The only data we may still hold is anonymous crash reports from Apple/Google standard crash reporting, which contain no personal identifiers.

4. Can't open the app?

If you can't reach the in-app option, email us and we'll delete your account for you:

  • Email: info@nubric.dev
  • Subject line: Sixty4 Racing Account Deletion
  • Include the email address you signed in with (Apple or Google)

We will complete the deletion and respond within 30 days.
