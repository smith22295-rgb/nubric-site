Sixty4 Racing

Clearing Your Data

Effective: June 7, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 7, 2026

Sixty4 Racing has no sign-in, no user profile, and no account — so there is no account to delete. We collect no names, emails, or personal identifiers. The only data you can clear is what's stored locally on your device.

1. Delete local data in the app

  1. Open Sixty4 Racing on your phone
  2. Go to Settings → Your data
  3. Tap Delete local data and confirm

This erases your garage (cars, runs, lap and elapsed-time records), your device list, your preferences, and the references to any car photos you added — all in one step. It can't be undone.

2. Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Sixty4 Racing removes all local data automatically. You can revoke Bluetooth or Photos permission at any time through your device's system settings.

3. What's stored on our servers

Because Sixty4 Racing has no accounts and no cloud sync, there is no personal data tied to you on our servers. The only data we ever receive is anonymous crash reports from Apple/Google standard crash reporting, which contain no personal identifiers.

4. Requesting deletion of crash logs

If you want us to delete crash logs your device sent us (within the platform retention window), email us:

  • Email: info@nubric.dev
  • Subject line: Sixty4 Racing Data Request
  • Include your device model and the approximate date range

We will respond within 30 days.
